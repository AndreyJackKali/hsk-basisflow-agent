Slide 2 / 8 · The Problem

Why Autonomous AI Trading Fails in DeFi Today

1. Blind Private-Key Custody

Most AI bots demand raw wallet private keys. One hallucination or prompt injection drains 100% of user funds.

2. Off-Chain Only "Guardrails"

Risk caps written in Python/JS vanish when an LLM loops, APIs glitch, or liquidity gaps during a flash crash.

3. Unauditable Decisions

Traders cannot verify why an AI agent entered a swap or prove that slippage and drawdown rules were enforced.

BasisFlow Agent solves all three by moving enforcement on-chain to HashKey Chain.

Slide 3 / 8 · Architecture

Non-Custodial AgentVault.sol + HSKSwapV3Adapter.sol

Role Separation

Owner deposits/withdraws USDT, sets risk limits, and holds emergencyUnwind(). Agent EOA can ONLY call trade() and can NEVER withdraw.

7 Hard On-Chain Guardrails

  • maxTradeQuote & maxPositionBps
  • maxSlippageBps vs live quote
  • cooldownSecs & maxDailyTrades
  • dailyLossCapQuote & hard stopLossEquity

Permissionless Circuit Breaker

Anyone can call triggerStopLoss() if marked equity drops below stopLossEquity, freezing the agent instantaneously.

Every trade stores TradeRecord{id, keccak256(signalJSON), buyBase, amountIn, amountOut, equityAfter, timestamp}.
